A field guide to the objectives that train modern neural networks.

Every model learns by minimizing a number. Which number you choose decides what the model becomes. This atlas sorts the loss functions you'll actually meet into seven families — start from what your model is doing, follow the branch, read the formula.

How to read this · and where your notes fit

Your three categories map cleanly onto this map. Reconstruction loss lives in the Regression family (pixel-level: MSE/MAE) and the Generative family (feature-level: Perceptual loss). Regularization loss is the KL term in the Generative family. Contrastive loss is the whole Metric / Embedding family.
